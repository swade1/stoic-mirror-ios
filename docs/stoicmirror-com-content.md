## Why Stoicism (positioning section)

The Stoics believed wisdom begins with seeing yourself clearly — like looking into a mirror. That's why Marcus Aurelius kept a private journal we know today as *Meditations*. He never intended it for anyone else to read; it was just a Roman emperor, alone, trying to think clearly under pressure.

The Stoic Mirror isn't therapy, and it isn't a quote-of-the-day app. It's a practice: naming what's actually bothering you, and letting people who thought seriously about exactly that problem — Marcus Aurelius on burdens of duty, Seneca on loss, Epictetus on what is and isn't yours to control — meet you where you are.

## FAQ

**Is this therapy?**
No. The Stoic Mirror is a personal reflection tool, not a medical or mental health service, and isn't a substitute for professional care. If you're in crisis, please contact a qualified professional or emergency services.

**Is the content real Stoic philosophy, or AI-generated quotes?**
The passages come from the actual Stoic texts, which are in the public domain. AI is used to match your concern to relevant passages and provide interpretation — not to invent quotes.

**What philosophers are included?**
Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, and Seneca, to start.

**Can I use it for free?**
Yes, with a 7-day free trial. See Pricing above.

THE STOIC MIRROR — ONBOARDING SCREENS
======================================

SCREEN 1 — The Problem
------------------------
Headline: "Your mind won't stop."

Body:
You keep thinking about it. The conversation that went wrong. The thing you can't fix. The future you can't predict. No matter how hard you try, your thoughts keep circling back.

You're not broken. You're human. But there's a better way.

The Stoics believed that wisdom begins with seeing yourself clearly — like looking into a mirror. Not to judge what you see, but to understand it. That's why Marcus Aurelius kept a journal we know today as Meditations. That's why this app is called The Stoic Mirror.


SCREEN 2 — The Insight
------------------------
Headline: "Some things are up to you. Most things aren't."

Body:
Two thousand years ago, a former slave named Epictetus learned something that changed everything: the source of all anxiety is trying to control what was never ours to control.

Other people's opinions. The past. The future. What happens to us.

None of it is ours. And the moment we stop trying to control it, something remarkable happens.

A century later, an emperor chose the same path.


SCREEN 3 — The Practice
------------------------
Headline: "Peace of mind isn't found. It's practiced."

Body:
The Stoics didn't just think about these ideas. They practiced them — daily, deliberately, through self-examination and reflection.

Marcus Aurelius kept a journal. Seneca reviewed his day every evening. Epictetus taught his students to examine every thought.

This is that practice. For your life. For your concerns. For today.


SCREEN 4 — The Tool
--------------------
Headline: "Tell the philosophers what's troubling you."

Body:
Describe what's on your mind — a relationship, a fear, a decision, a frustration. The Stoic Mirror searches thousands of passages from Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, and Seneca and returns their exact words, matched to your specific concern.

Not generic advice. Their actual wisdom. For your actual life.


SCREEN 5 — The Promise
-----------------------
Headline: "You are more powerful than you think."

Body:
The Stoics had a word for what happens when you stop fighting what you can't control and start mastering what you can: equanimity. A quiet, unshakeable calm that no circumstance can touch.

It doesn't happen overnight. But it starts here. With one concern. One reflection. One ancient voice that knew exactly what you're going through.
